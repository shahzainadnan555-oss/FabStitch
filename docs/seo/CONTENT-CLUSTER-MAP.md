## Missing angles

The ontology has fibre notes, garment constraints, attribute definitions, and two construction notes. It does not yet have a separate paragraph for each material-use pair. Until those exist, combination pages will keep quoting the parent note. Do not invent GSM ranges or test results to fill that gap.

## Balance

Material-use, material-attribute, use-attribute, and marketplace topic families are the large clusters. Guides, questions, comparisons, and sourcing pages are the smaller, more distinct clusters. Do not add another thousand combination URLs.
