## What was not changed, and why

Fabric product pages already quote the published composition, construction, and weight. Adding a GSM, a certificate, a country, or an MOQ would be a fabricated spec.

Discover and marketplace combination notes are supporting pages. Giving each one a long original essay would multiply near-duplicate articles. They keep a short, pairing-specific note.

Location, certification, and MOQ keywords are mapped only as secondary language on a page that can discuss the idea honestly. They are not turned into local or certificate landing pages.
